The Problem

The Cost of Traditional Residence Maintenance is Bleeding Profits.

1. Disastrous Unreported Leaks & Damages

Students ignore small leaks or broken fixtures because reporting is inconvenient. By the time maintenance finds out, ceilings have collapsed or geysers have burst, costing R80k–R300k in emergency repairs.

2. Contractor Over-Invoicing & Phantom Work

External plumbing and electrical contractors bill for hours and parts with zero verifiable proof. Residence owners pay invoices blindly without knowing if the tenant's socket or tap was actually fixed.

3. WhatsApp & Paper Logbook Chaos

Reception staff get drowned in conflicting WhatsApp messages, sticky notes, and student desk queues. Tickets get lost, staff burn out, and response times stretch to weeks.

4. Reputation Damage & NSFAS Accreditation Risk

Frustrated students take to TikTok, Twitter, and University Housing Committees. A single hygiene or electrical hazard report can trigger DHET audit flags or forfeit university leasing accreditation.

The Game-Changer

The "Closed Only When Fixed" Guarantee.

In traditional residences, maintenance marks a ticket "done" while the student's geyser is still leaking. Our system introduces a two-key closing mechanism:

Student Confirmation Sign-off

Once a technician marks a ticket Resolved, the student receives an in-app verification prompt: "Has your issue been fully resolved to your satisfaction?" If confirmed, the ticket is permanently closed.

Reopen Loop with Reason Logging

If the repair was botched, the student clicks "Reopen Request" and enters mandatory feedback. The ticket bounces straight back to "In Progress", notifying the Facilities Director instantly.

Legal & Audit Protection: Every single status shift, comment, technician assignment, and student confirmation is saved to an immutable audit log that can be printed for board meetings, insurance claims, or DHET housing reviews.

Modern Technology

Play Store Native App & Smart QR Room Door Tagging.

APP

Google Play Store & Mobile Deployment

Students and staff install the branded app directly on their Android devices. Native push notifications wake technicians instantly during emergencies, even when the app is closed.

  • • Offline-first design: works smoothly in basement corridors and poor Wi-Fi zones.
  • • Direct camera access for snap-and-upload fault photos.
QR

Smart QR Room Tagging

Every bedroom and communal facility receives a durable, branded QR sticker.

A resident simply points their phone camera at their door sticker: the app opens with their Building Block (e.g. Block B) and Room Number (e.g. 204) already pre-populated.

Eliminates 100% of "wrong room number" or "which block are you in?" dispatch errors!
Compliance

Enterprise Security & Full POPIA Data Privacy.

Confidential Staff Passcode

Staff registration is gated behind a confidential authorization code (CORRIDOR-STAFF-5849) with strict masking and anti-autofill hygiene. Students can never elevate their own privileges.

POPIA & Student Isolation

Complies with the South African Protection of Personal Information Act. Resident phone numbers, student numbers, and personal details are strictly isolated from other students.

Zero Plaintext Passwords

All user credentials utilize browser Web Crypto salted SHA-256 password hashing. Zero API keys, anon tokens, or database passwords are ever exposed on the client.
